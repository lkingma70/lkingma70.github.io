Inventory stays private. Approved buyers browse; the public sees this page, not the horses. Every check is synthetic and clearly labeled - nothing here implies health, soundness, title, or suitability.

How it works

  1. 01

    Know what was checked

    Every listing carries a claim-level Evidence Passport with source, date, status, and an explicit limitation.

  2. 02

    Know who is authorized

    Identity and authority-to-list are shown as separate items, never a single verified badge.

  3. 03

    Send serious inquiries

    A structured inquiry names the buyer, their trainer, and the buyer's real intent for this horse.

  4. 04

    Contact opens after acceptance or price

    Details stay masked until the seller accepts or provides a price. The demo does not promise a response time.

For buyers

  • See the Evidence Passport up front - each claim shows who checked it, when, and its explicit limitation.
  • See when the seller last confirmed the horse is still available, and how long ago that was.
  • Send one qualified inquiry that names your trainer and your intent; contact stays masked until the seller accepts or provides a price.

For sellers & trainers

  • Choose exact price, a range, or price on inquiry - per horse.
  • Control who sees each listing by buyer qualification.
  • See serious buyer + trainer context and a stated intent before releasing contact details.
